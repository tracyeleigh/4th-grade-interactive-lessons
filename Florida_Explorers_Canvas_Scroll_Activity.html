🌴 Explorers Who Came to Florida ⛵

Florida Files • Grade 4 Social Studies • New Spain and Exploration

Big Question: Who were the first Europeans to come to Florida, and how can historians understand how their arrival changed Florida's story?

Mission: Complete each activity to become a Florida History Detective!

Put the three expeditions in order from earliest to latest.

1513: Juan Ponce de León reached Florida.

1539: Hernando de Soto landed near Tampa Bay.

⛵ Activity 2: Explorer Match-Up

Match each explorer to the clue.

🔎 Activity 3: Source Detective

Map clues: A Spanish map from 1584—about 45 years after de Soto landed—shows Native towns across La Florida and small red buildings.

1. What does the map tell us about Florida?

2. Why is a map useful to a historian?

⚖️ Activity 4: First Contact Sort

Choose the category that best fits each event.

✍️ Activity 5: Historian's Notebook

Prompt: Name one Spanish explorer who came to Florida. Explain when he came and one way his expedition affected the people who already lived here.

Sentence starters:
  • In ______, ______ came to Florida.
  • His expedition was important because…
  • For the Native peoples already living in Florida, first contact meant…

🏁 Exit Ticket

Why should historians tell the story of Florida exploration from more than one perspective?
